Privacy

Privacy Policy

Tint Lab for iPhone and Android · Effective 10 November 2026

The short version. Tint Lab has no account and no servers of its own. Your saved game (levels, coins and settings) stays on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect some device and usage data to serve and measure ads. To fix bugs and make levels better, the app also sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Firebase, and crash reports, performance data and screen recordings of app sessions (session replay) to Luciq. None of this includes your name, and nothing is sold. On iPhone, your advertising identifier is shared only if you allow tracking.

Who we are

Tint Lab ("Tint Lab: Water Sort Puzzle") is made by Mohamed ElZoghbi, publishing as ZaPlatform ("we", "us"). You can reach us at zaplatform@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Tint Lab saves your game on your device. We don't keep a copy of your saved game on any server. (The anonymous statistics and diagnostics described under Analytics and diagnostics are separate.)

There is no sign-in, no account and no sync between devices. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Settings → Reset progress clears your progress at any time.

Advertising: Google AdMob

Tint Lab is free because it shows ads: a banner on the Home screen, a full-screen ad between some levels after level 10, and optional reward ads that play only when you choose them. These are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and consent is handled with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Unless you've bought Remove ads or Premium, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers. On iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (see below). On Android, the Android advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone's settings. Plus other device and app identifiers Google uses for ads.
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your coarse location (such as your country or city).
  • Usage and diagnostic data, such as app launches, ad load times, performance and crash data related to ads.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped one.

Google uses this data to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. Where you allow it, ads may also be personalised. Google processes this data under its own policy, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

We don't receive the data Google collects about you. We see only totals in our AdMob account, such as the number of ads shown and earnings.

Tracking permission on iPhone (App Tracking Transparency)

On iPhone, Tint Lab asks whether it may track you before any advertising identifier is used. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared with Google, and you'll still see ads, just not ones based on your activity in other apps and websites. You can change your choice at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Advertising ID on Android

On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or delete it (opt out of personalised ads) in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Tint Lab shows Google's consent message before ads are personalised, and you can accept, refuse or choose by purpose. To review or change your choice later, open Tint Lab and go to Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Purchases

Remove ads and Premium are one-time in-app purchases processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Billing). We never see your payment details, your Apple Account or your Google account. The app learns only which purchases you own. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics and diagnostics

To understand which levels are too hard, find crashes and improve the game, Tint Lab uses two services. Neither is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell this data.

Google Firebase (Analytics and Remote Config). The app sends gameplay events such as level started or finished, number of moves, time taken, use of undo or the extra bottle, ads offered or watched, and purchases started or completed, together with a random app-instance ID, device model, OS version, app version, language and approximate country. Firebase Analytics on iPhone does not use your advertising identifier. Remote Config lets us switch app features on or off without an update. See the Firebase privacy information.

Luciq (crash reporting, performance and bug reporting). Luciq receives:

  • Crash and performance data, such as crash logs, app hangs, launch and screen loading times, device model, OS version and free memory.
  • Session replay: low-frame-rate screen recordings of what happens inside Tint Lab during a session (the game board, taps and screens), with a log of the steps taken. Session replay only records Tint Lab's own screens, never other apps, and text fields are hidden.
  • Bug reports and feedback you choose to send, by shaking your phone or taking a screenshot: your message, a screenshot, any photos, videos or voice notes you attach, and an email address if you enter one so we can reply.

Luciq processes this data for us as a service provider and keeps it for a limited time. See the Luciq Privacy Policy. Tint Lab sends no notifications.

Children

Tint Lab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Your choices

  • iPhone: refuse or withdraw tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's ad settings.
  • In the EEA, the UK or Switzerland: Settings → Privacy & ad choices in Tint Lab.
  • Stop ads with the one-time Remove ads or Premium purchase.
  • Manage how Google personalises ads at My Ad Center.
  • Delete everything Tint Lab stores by deleting the app.
  • Ask us to delete analytics, diagnostics or bug reports linked to your device or email: write to zaplatform@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
